[#1] Afterword
[#2] When I started drawing Chapter 1,
I hoped I could make it as far as
Grandmother's introduction.

More than anything, I always wanted
to portray Tadashi as the one person
who was saved precisely because Tsubaki
was a sexually frustrated wild young lady.
I'm truly glad I made it that far.

I secretly set a goal of ending the series
in ten volumes, with five volumes about
their relationship before they started dating
and five after. Both halves are equally
important to me.
The next volume will finally be the last.

We could only make it this far
thanks to all our readers.
I'll make the final volume an enjoyable one,
so please continue watching over
these two until the very end.
